When Seth uses Heat Snap and Spinning Blades, the camera enters a parallax mode. In my humble opinion, i think this feature adds variety to boss fights. I hope you like this idea, especially the developer of the Split mod. The mod includes fine-tuning options for the parallax effect, so everyone can customize it to their liking
If this feature appears in the original mod, this mod will be removed from the Workshop
